## Our Firm's Standards

### Reporting
- Accounting standard: IFRS
- Reporting currency: USD
- Fiscal year end: December 31

### Variance Analysis
- Calculate as: (Actual − Budget) ÷ Budget × 100%
- Flag anything over ±10% as material
- Always show: Category | Budget | Actual | Variance %

### CFO Commentary Style
- Formal, concise tone — max 200 words
- Active voice: "Revenue increased" not "There was an increase"
- Always cover: Revenue → Costs → EBITDA → Outlook
